Privacy

Announced 25 September 2026, here, with a dated link in the hub's footer: an optional Maître d' can read a menu for you if you add your own Anthropic key, and nothing is sent without it. In force from 26 September 2026; announced 25 September 2026. Until then nothing on this site could send what you write anywhere. The whole policy fits on this page.

The short version. Everything runs on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you add your own Anthropic key and press a button that says what it sends. Then only what you put in the box, the photograph you chose, or the address you typed goes to Anthropic, under Anthropic's terms, and your key stays on this device and never rides in an export. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no payment of any kind to us, so there is nothing about you to store: no scores, no answers, not a word you wrote. There is no analytics service, no advertising, and nothing to sell even if we wanted to. The short list of third parties below covers the few things a page fetches, or sends, when you ask it to.

Your study work

Every wing on this site runs in your browser. Progress, notes, settings, bookmarks, journals and vault entries are stored in your browser's local storage, on your own device. What you write (journal entries, tasting notes, saved menus, anything in a vault) is not uploaded on its own: the one way anything leaves is the Maître d', and she sends only what you put in front of her, the menu you paste or photograph, or the menu you have entered when you ask her about it, and only when you press the button. The wings that hold your writing give you export tools, and those exports go where you put them and nowhere else.

Your account

There isn't one. Outside Of Time Hospitality is free and has no sign-in and no password to lose. Nobody has a record that you use it, because no such record is ever made. The five apps ask for nothing and send nothing on their own. There is one door, and it is yours to open: the Maître d', who reads a menu for you and answers questions about it, works only once you add your own Anthropic key, and sends something only when you press a button that says what it sends. Then only what you put in the box, the photograph you chose, or the address you typed goes to Anthropic, under Anthropic's terms, and your key stays on this device and never rides in an export. The letter signup and the trip-inquiry form that Calendar For Life used to carry have been removed, so there is no list to join and nothing that asks for an email address; what you choose to type into the Maître d's box goes to Anthropic like anything else you put there.

Calendar For Life keeps its own policy for what it stores on your device, which this page does not shrink.

These apps ask for no name. Each one keeps a single record on the device it is installed on, and nothing that leaves the device carries a name, because there is none to carry. If a paid edition ever returns and you choose to sign in, this page will change before anything about that changes.

Your results

Scores, streaks, exam records and tasting notes are written to your browser's local storage and stay there; the one thing a wing remembers that can leave is a menu or list you entered, and only when you hand it to the Maître d' (see Anthropic below). Nothing is uploaded when you finish a round, online or off. If you clear your browser's data for this site, it is gone: there is no copy anywhere else, which is the other half of the same promise.

Paying

There is no payment to us. No checkout, no card, no processor. The one thing that can cost you money is the Maître d', and only on your own Anthropic key, billed by Anthropic under Anthropic's terms. The app shows an estimate before every run and refuses, on this device, any run its estimate says would pass the monthly figure you set. The figures are the app's own reckoning, and your Anthropic bill is the truth.

Every third party, listed

  • YouTube: two wings show video from YouTube. First Light's practice page draws its video titles and thumbnails live the moment you open it. The Bartender's Ledger pins a film to some of its Coffee & Tea lessons and contacts YouTube only for the lesson you have actually opened: one thumbnail, and one lookup to confirm the film still plays. On either wing, pressing play loads YouTube's embedded player. YouTube sees those requests; nothing about your account goes with them.
  • Google Fonts: Calendar For Life loads its typefaces from Google, which sees the standard request data. The hub and every other wing ship their own fonts (licence), so no font request leaves your device.
  • Anthropic: the Maître d' is a language model at Anthropic, and the only thing on this site that can carry your own material anywhere. She works only with an Anthropic key you add yourself, and sends only when you press a button that says what it sends. What goes: the text you put in the box, the photograph or PDF you chose, the address you typed, the menu items you ask her to fill in or write lines for, as you have entered them, and, when you ask her a question, your menu as you have entered it (name, section, description, confirmed ingredients, price and confirmed dietary marks), plus, on this site, your wine list and bar list, so she can answer about the whole house; and your own key as the credential. What does not go: nothing on its own; no allergen marks, because she is never shown one and the app refuses any she tries to write; nothing from the other wings except those two lists, and only with a question; and the key never rides in an export. Anthropic's own privacy terms govern what it does with what it receives. The key is yours, stored only on this device, and forgotten the moment you say so. Before every request the app shows an estimate and refuses, on this device, any request its estimate says would pass the monthly figure you set; it keeps a readable ledger of what each request cost, by its own reckoning, and your Anthropic bill is the truth.
  • jsDelivr: nothing is fetched from it in this edition. It is named in one file, for a sign-in library, and that file never loads it because this edition has no sign-in to load it for. It is listed here because it would be contacted if that ever changed, and this page would say so first.
    Reading a menu off a photograph used to download a text reader from jsDelivr. It no longer does: that download was removed. Your own browser reads the photograph where it can, or not at all; if you add your own key and press the button that says so, the Maître d' reads it, and then the photograph goes to Anthropic as the Anthropic entry above says. The photograph was never uploaded then, and now it leaves this device only when you send it to her.

That is the whole list. No analytics service, no advertising network, no tracking pixels, no social buttons.

Your data, your call

Everything lives in your browser's local storage, so it is already in your hands: clearing this site's data removes all of it, and there is no server copy of ours to ask about. If you used the Maître d', Anthropic may hold what you sent it, under Anthropic's terms; we never see it and cannot fetch it back. If you have a question anyway, write to zpullen98@gmail.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change is said out loud on the hub before it takes effect.
