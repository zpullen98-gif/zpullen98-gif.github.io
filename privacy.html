Privacy

In force from 29 August 2026. The whole policy fits on this page.

The short version. Everything runs on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no payment of any kind, so there is nothing about you to store: no scores, no answers, not a word you wrote. There is no analytics service, no advertising, and nothing to sell even if we wanted to. The short list of third parties below covers the few things a page fetches when you ask it to.

Your study work

Every wing on this site runs in your browser. Progress, notes, settings, bookmarks, journals and vault entries are stored in your browser's local storage, on your own device. What you write (journal entries, tasting notes, saved menus, anything in a vault) is never uploaded. The wings that hold your writing give you export tools, and those exports go where you put them and nowhere else.

Your account

There isn't one. Outside Of Time is free and has no sign-in, no email list and no password to lose. Nobody has a record that you use it, because no such record is ever made.

Your results

Scores, streaks, exam records, tasting notes and everything else a wing remembers are written to your browser's local storage and stay there. Nothing is uploaded when you finish a round, online or off. If you clear your browser's data for this site, it is gone: there is no copy anywhere else, which is the other half of the same promise.

Paying

There is no payment. No checkout, no card, no processor.

Every third party, listed

  • Web3Forms: carries the Calendar For Life newsletter and trip-inquiry forms when you submit one. You have to ask; opening the page sends nothing. That wing keeps its own policy, which this page does not shrink.
  • YouTube — First Light's practice page draws its video titles and thumbnails live from YouTube the moment you open it, and playing a practice loads YouTube's embedded player. YouTube sees those requests; nothing about your account goes with them.
  • Google Fonts — Calendar For Life loads its typefaces from Google, which sees the standard request data. Every other wing ships its own fonts (licence), so no font request leaves your device.

That is the whole list. No analytics service, no advertising network, no tracking pixels, no social buttons.

Your data, your call

Everything lives in your browser's local storage, so it is already in your hands: clearing this site's data removes all of it, and there is no server copy to ask about. If you have a question anyway, write to zpullen98@gmail.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change is said out loud on the hub before it takes effect.
