  1. Responsible Service & the Law Cocktail & Bar

    The module's non-negotiable close, because the license IS the business. DRAM SHOP LIABILITY: in most US states (and analogues elsewhere), a bar can be held civilly liable for damages caused by a guest it OVERSERVED; the legal stakes of 'one more' are existential, not theoretical. The operational duties: TIPS/ServSafe-Alcohol-style certification for every pourer (many jurisdictions mandate it; smart owners do regardless), ID verification culture (check everyone who could plausibly be under 30; know your fake-spotting; the fine and license suspension outcost the awkwardness forever), cut-off protocols TRAINED and role-played (the intervention script: slow, water, food, then a clear kind no with a manager's backup, never a public showdown), and transportation care (the cab/ride offer is hospitality and mitigation in one). The culture note: pacing IS hospitality: water back-poured automatically, food pushed generously (the aperitivo entry's snack economics doubling as safety), sessionable options genuinely promoted (the spritz and NA entries as harm reduction wearing a good outfit). The owner's summary: every entry in this module builds revenue; this one keeps it: serve like the license, the guest's night, and someone's drive home all depend on the next pour, because all three do.
