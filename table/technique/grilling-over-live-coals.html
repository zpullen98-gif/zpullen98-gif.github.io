Grilling over live coals

77 dishes across 53 chapters · 0 marked cooked

On the Path of Study: Semester 7: Fire & Smoke, Semester 9: Seafood Mastery

From the Lexicon, Grilling vs. Barbecue vs. Smoking

Three fires, three clocks. GRILLING: direct high heat, minutes, for tender cuts: the two-zone setup (hot side/cool side) is the entire skill, giving sear and refuge in one grate. BARBECUE: indirect low heat (105–135°C) plus wood smoke, hours, for collagen cuts (brisket, shoulder, ribs) where 'low and slow' is the collagen conversion clock wearing an apron. SMOKING divides again: hot-smoking cooks while flavoring; cold-smoking (under ~30°C) flavors cured foods without cooking. Thin blue smoke good, white billows bitter; the fire is an ingredient and must be cooked as carefully as the meat.

Read it in the Lexicon →

How to tell it is going right

  • The fire was ready before the food was: coals evenly covered in grey ash with no flames licking through and no raw smoke coming off, and a hot zone and a cooler one both available.
  • The grate was hot and clean when the food landed, and the food released cleanly from it when the time came to turn rather than tearing away.
  • Marks are browned, not burnt: deep colour where the metal touched, with no carbon flaking off and no soot transferred onto the food.
  • The interior reaches its target with a thin gradient, not charred outside and cold within, and not driven through by a flare-up.
  • What comes off the fire smells of fire and food only: no raw wood smoke, no fuel, nothing acrid at the back of the nose.

Where it goes wrong It went on over live flames instead of settled coals, so the outside charred bitter in the flare-ups while the middle stayed cold, and everything on the plate tastes of soot.

A chosen film Setting Up a Two-Zone Charcoal Fire | Weber Grills Weber Grills Watch for: The coals being banked to one side and nothing else: the hot side and the empty refuge beside it are the whole technique, and seeing the actual depth of the coal bed is what a diagram cannot teach.
