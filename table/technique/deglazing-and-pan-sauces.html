From the Lexicon, Deglaze & Fond

FOND (French for 'base/foundation') is the browned glaze of proteins and sugars welded to the pan after searing, concentrated Maillard flavor that amateurs scrub into the sink and professionals convert into money. DEGLAZING: pouring liquid (wine, stock, even water) into the hot emptied pan and scraping; the fond dissolves into an instant sauce base. From there: reduce, season, mount with butter, and a sautéed anything becomes a finished dish. The habit to build: never wash a pan with fond in it until you've asked what sauce it wanted to become.
