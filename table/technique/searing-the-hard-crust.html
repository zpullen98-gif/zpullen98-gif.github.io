From the Lexicon, Searing & Pan Technique

The skill inside the skill: preheat the pan dry until oil shimmers instantly, dry the protein aggressively, salt just before, lay it away from you, and then DO NOT MOVE IT: proteins release themselves when the crust forms and tear when rushed. Fond, the browned layer stuck to the pan, is not mess but the future sauce; deglaze with wine or stock and scrape. Baste with foaming butter, garlic, and thyme in the final minute (arroser) for the restaurant finish. Crowding is the cardinal sin: steam kills sear; work in batches like your Michelin star depends on it.
