On the Path of Study: Semester 8: The Wok & High Heat

From the Lexicon, Wok Hei & Stir-Fry Discipline

Wok hei, 'the breath of the wok', is the smoky, singed-air aroma from food and oil vaporizing against carbon steel at extreme heat, micro-charring in mid-toss. The home translation: maximum burner, a carbon-steel wok preheated to smoking, ingredients DRY and cut uniform, everything prepped before ignition (a stir-fry is 90% mise, 10% cooking), small batches, sauces poured down the hot wok wall so they scorch-caramelize on arrival, and the discipline of letting food SIT between tosses to catch char. Crowding is the killer; one portion at a time is the law from char kway teow to pad see ew.
