From the Lexicon, Steaming & En Papillote

Cooking in vapor at 100°C: gentler than boiling (no agitation, no flavor leached into water) and the technique of choice where the ingredient IS the dish: Cantonese fish, dim sum, couscous, tamales, Christmas pudding. EN PAPILLOTE seals food in parchment so it steams in its own moisture plus wine and butter, a self-saucing micro-oven with tableside theater. Steam burns worse than boiling water (it releases latent heat on contact); open lids and packets away from yourself, always, and never let the pot run dry: the smell of scorched steamer is a lesson learned once.
