The bare simmer: holding liquid below the boil

59 dishes across 43 chapters · 0 marked cooked

On the Path of Study: Semester 4: The Braise, Semester 6: Pastry Fundamentals

From the Lexicon, Poach, Simmer, Boil

Three temperatures pretending to be one method. POACH: 71–82°C, water barely shivering: eggs, fish, chicken breast, anything delicate that violent bubbles would shred. SIMMER: 85–95°C, lazy bubbles: stocks, braises, beans, sauces. BOIL: 100°C rolling: pasta, blanching, potatoes, and almost nothing made of protein. The most common home error is treating these as synonyms; the most common professional discipline is watching the bubbles, not the dial. Salt poaching liquids and courts-bouillons like they are the sauce, because for those minutes they are.
