From the Lexicon, Knife Cuts: The Classical Ladder

The French system exists because even cuts cook evenly and plate professionally. The ladder: BRUNOISE (3mm dice) and its baby FINE BRUNOISE (1.5mm); JULIENNE (matchsticks 3mm × 5cm) and BATONNET (6mm × 6cm, the fry); small/medium/large DICE (6/12/20mm); PAYSANNE (thin tiles); CHIFFONADE (herbs and leaves rolled and sliced into ribbons); TOURNÉ (the seven-sided football that has hazed culinary students for a century). MINCE is fineness without geometry; SUPREME is the citrus segment freed from all membrane. Speed comes later; uniformity is the exam, and the claw grip is the tuition.
