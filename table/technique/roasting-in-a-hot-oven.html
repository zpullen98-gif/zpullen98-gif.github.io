Roasting in a hot oven

49 dishes across 40 chapters · 0 marked cooked

On the Path of Study: Semester 7: Fire & Smoke, Semester 10: The Restaurateur's Capstone

From the Lexicon, Roasting, Baking & Carryover

Dry ambient heat in an enclosed oven: 'roasting' for meats and vegetables, 'baking' for doughs, the physics identical. The professional's three levers: TEMPERATURE STRATEGY (high-then-low for a crusted roast, low-then-blast 'reverse sear' for even doneness plus crust), AIRFLOW (convection browns faster and dries surfaces: drop 15°C from conventional recipes), and CARRYOVER: internal temperature climbs 3–8°C after removal as the exterior's heat equalizes inward. Pull the roast BEFORE target and rest it: resting redistributes juices and finishes the cook. Cutting early spills what patience would have kept.
