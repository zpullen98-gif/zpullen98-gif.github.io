Skimming: foam, fat and a clear broth

20 dishes across 17 chapters · 0 marked cooked

On the Path of Study: Semester 4: The Braise, Semester 10: The Restaurateur's Capstone

From the Lexicon, Consommé & Clarification

Stock clarified to crystal transparency using a RAFT: egg whites, ground meat, and mirepoix whisked into cold stock, then heated gently: the proteins coagulate into a floating filter that captures every particle while the meat re-fortifies flavor. Ladled out through the raft's vent hole, the result is a soup so clear you read the bowl's maker's mark, with more flavor than it has any right to. The modern shortcuts (ice filtration through a frozen block, agar clarification) earn their place, but the raft remains the classical exam: patience, temperature control, and protein chemistry in one pot.
