Draining fried food: a rack, never paper

22 dishes across 19 chapters · 0 marked cooked

On the Path of Study: Semester 9: Seafood Mastery

From the Lexicon, Fryer Setups & Oil Management

Frying's equipment truth: the vessel is simple; the DISCIPLINE is the machine. Home setups ranked: a heavy DUTCH OVEN with a clip thermometer (thermal mass = temperature stability, the recovery physics of the frying entry) beats most countertop electric fryers (underpowered, slow recovery); a WOK fries with less oil and more surface; dedicated electric fryers earn space only with filtration and frequency. The management protocol that separates operations from accidents: never past two-thirds full, food DRY (water is the eruption), a spider and a landing rack staged BEFORE the first drop, baking soda or a lid for fire (never water: say it twice), and oil husbandry: strain through fine mesh after each session, store dark and cool, track uses by what was fried (fish retires oil fastest), retire at darkening, foaming, or off smells. Air fryers, honestly filed: compact convection ovens: excellent at reheating fried food and roasting small batches, incapable of actual frying; useful appliance, dishonest name. Restaurant note: fryer oil is a tracked COST line and a fire-code fixture: respect it at home like the pros are required to.
