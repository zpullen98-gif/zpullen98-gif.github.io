Egg wash & the baker’s shine

29 dishes across 24 chapters · 0 marked cooked

From the Lexicon, Baste, Arroser & Glaze

Three ways of painting with heat. BASTING: spooning pan juices or fat over roasting food: flavor and sheen, though evidence says its moisture claims are romance; it mostly speeds surface cooking and pleases the cook. ARROSER: the French pan ritual: tilting the pan and spoon-showering foaming butter, garlic, and thyme over a steak or fish in its final minute: aroma infusion plus a butterfat crust. GLAZING: painting reduced sauces (teriyaki, barbecue, honey-mustard) in THIN repeated coats late in cooking so sugars lacquer instead of burn, the huli huli and yakitori law. Sugar burns at the temperature meat browns; timing is the technique.
