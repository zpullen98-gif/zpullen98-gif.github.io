Churning ice cream

3 dishes across 3 chapters · 0 marked cooked

From the Lexicon, Frozen Dessert Machines: Churners & Pacojet

Ice cream is texture engineering, small ice crystals plus controlled air (overrun), and the machines are crystal-size strategies. HOME CHURNERS: frozen-bowl (cheap, one batch, pre-freeze the bowl 24h) vs. self-refrigerating compressors (batch after batch, the enthusiast's line). The pastry-science levers travel with either: sugar and alcohol depress freezing (scoopability), fat carries flavor, stabilizers (or the custard wing's egg base) manage melt; churn cold bases fast, harden in shallow containers. The pro disruptors: the PACOJET spins FROZEN solid blocks into instant micro-shaved creams to order (savory sorbets, herb ices, single-portion economics, a fine-dining fixture), and its consumer descendant (the Ninja Creami class) brought the same shave-don't-churn idea home, remaking the category. Kulfi (the dessert wing) reminds everyone the no-machine road exists: density as a choice. Whatever the hardware: the base recipe is 80% of the result: machines only preserve the texture you formulated.
