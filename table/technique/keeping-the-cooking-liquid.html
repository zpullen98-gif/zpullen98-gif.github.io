Keeping the cooking liquid

26 dishes across 24 chapters · 0 marked cooked

On the Path of Study: Semester 3: The Sauté Station & Pan Sauces, Semester 9: Seafood Mastery

From the Lexicon, Stocks: Blanc, Brun, Broth & Demi

WHITE STOCK: raw bones, gentle simmer: clean, pale, for velouté and light soups. BROWN STOCK: bones and mirepoix roasted first, tomato paste caramelized: deep color and roasted flavor, the mother of demi-glace and jus. BROTH is meat-based and seasoned to drink; stock is bone-based, unsalted, and built for gelatin. A proper stock sets like Jell-O cold. GLACE is stock reduced to a syrup; a spoon flavors a whole pan sauce. Skim relentlessly, never boil (cloudiness is emulsified scum), salt never (it concentrates downstream), and know that stock is the bank account every other station borrows from.
