From the Lexicon, Resting, Carryover & the Thermometer

The instant-read thermometer is the cheapest skill upgrade in cooking: doneness is a temperature, not a time. Key coordinates: 50–52°C rare beef, 55–57 medium-rare, 63°C+rest for pork, 68–74°C poultry white-to-dark, 82°C custards, 95°C brisket-probe-tender. CARRYOVER adds 3–8°C after removal (more in big roasts); RESTING (5 min for steaks, 30–45 for large roasts) lets juices thicken and redistribute; meat cut immediately bleeds what patience would keep. The pros' phrase 'cook to temp, not to time' is the entire entry in five words; the pros' habit of probing the COLDEST likely spot is the footnote that matters.
