What the plate gets wrong

The picture was generated, and these are its errors of fact, checked against the kitchen and the Lexicon. Read the plate with them.

  • Figs prints “Figs, Season August to September”. Figs are a Mediterranean and California crop and not a Midwest harvest, so any fig on a Midwest menu is shipped in rather than picked locally. The common fig is not winter hardy across the twelve states on the plate's own map, and the app's Lexicon places the crop in Turkey, Greece, Italy, Spain, the Levant and California, with no Midwest orchard or market crop.
  • Black Raspberries prints “Season July to August”. Midwest black raspberries ripen from late June to mid July and are finished well before August, ahead of the red raspberries beside them. Black raspberries carry a single early summer crop about two weeks ahead of summer red raspberries, and Ohio, the leading black raspberry state, picks from late June to mid July.
  • Plantain prints “Plantain, Season August to October”. Plantains are a tropical cooking banana that cannot be grown in any Midwest state and have no Midwest season, so this card does not belong on a local harvest plate. Plantain is a frost tender tropical plant whose United States crop comes from Puerto Rico, Hawaii and imports, and the app's Lexicon teaches it as a vegetable staple of West Africa, the Caribbean and Latin America.
