Adjaruli Khachapuri

  • Intermediate
  • 1 h 50 m
  • $$ roughly 2 of 4 for cost
  • Vegetarian

rich · briny Leads with richness that coats and comforts, a briny, maritime salinity.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 350g flour, 200ml warm milk-water, 5g yeast, 1 tsp sugar, salt, oil
  • 300g cheese: mozzarella + feta + sulguni if the gods provide
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 30g butter
  • Nothing else needs to exist

Method

  1. Soft dough; rise 1 h. 60 min
  2. Roll ovals; pile cheese down the middle; roll the long sides in and twist the ends into a boat.
  3. Bake 240°C, 12–14 min until the crust bronzes and cheese bubbles. 14 min
  4. Crater the center; drop in a yolk and butter; return 1 min. Stir at the table; tear crust and drag. 1 min

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the technique it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Proofing: the rise before the oven 78 recipes

  • Judged on volume against the container rather than on the clock: visibly grown, and grown by roughly the amount the recipe describes.
  • The poke test agrees: a floured fingertip pressed a centimetre in leaves a dent that springs back slowly and only part of the way. Straight back means it is not ready; no spring at all means it has gone.
  • The surface is domed, smooth, and slightly sheened: not cratered, wrinkled, or collapsed, and not skinned over and dry from proofing uncovered.
  • Handled, it feels alive and airy: light for its size, with bubbles visible under the skin.
  • It springs in the oven: the piece lifts in the first minutes and the scores open. A shape that does not move at all was proofed past its point before it went in.

Where it goes wrong It was proofed to the clock in a kitchen colder or warmer than the recipe assumed: overproofed, it collapses as the blade touches it and bakes flat and dense with a sour edge; underproofed, it tears at the side and bakes tight.

From the pass

Georgia's cheese boat: the yolk cooks in residual heat as you stir it into the molten cheese with a chunk of butter, and the crust is your spoon. Tear from the pointed ends and drag through the middle. Sharing is technically possible. The Adjaruli, from the Black Sea coast, is shaped like the boats in its harbors; the sulguni-imeruli cheese blend is the target, approximated abroad with mozzarella-plus-feta. Georgia treats khachapuri seriously enough to track its price as an inflation index.
