Kansas Bran Muffins

  • Easy
  • 40 min
  • $$ roughly 2 of 4 for cost
  • Vegetarian

sweet-edged · rich Leads with a sweet edge rounding the corners, richness that coats and comforts.

Ingredients

  • 150g wheat bran
  • 250ml buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 60ml oil
  • 120ml molasses
  • 150g flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 100g raisins

Method

  1. Soak the bran in the buttermilk 15 min until softened. 15 min
  2. Beat in the egg, brown sugar, oil, and molasses.
  3. Whisk the flour, soda, and salt and fold in, then the raisins.
  4. Spoon into muffin cups.
  5. Bake at 190C for 18-20 min until risen and set. 20 min

From the pass

A wheat-country breakfast that puts the bran to use: Kansas grows the wheat, and the muffin is the state's daily monument to milling's leftovers. Soaking the bran in buttermilk first is essential: it softens the coarse flakes so the muffins are moist rather than gritty, and the batter actually improves after a night in the fridge: the old refrigerator-muffin recipes kept a crock of it ready for a week of breakfasts. Molasses for depth, raisins by household vote.
