Jiangnan · Main

Songshu Guiyu

  • Advanced
  • 45 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$

umami-deep · bright · nutty-toasted Leads with savory depth stacked from the pantry’s bass notes, acid holding everything up to the light, closing toasted and warm.

Ingredients

  • 1 mandarin fish (gui yu), about 700 g, scaled and gutted
  • 80 g potato starch
  • 20 ml Shaoxing wine
  • 5 g salt
  • 1.5 L neutral oil, for frying
  • 60 g caster sugar
  • 60 ml Zhenjiang black vinegar
  • 40 g tomato paste
  • 20 ml light soy sauce
  • 200 ml water
  • 30 g pine nuts, toasted
  • 40 g green peas
  • 40 g bamboo shoot, finely diced

Allergen screen

Found: gluten · nuts · fish · soy · alcohol

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Take the head off in one cut and keep it, then run the knife along the backbone to lift two fillets, leaving them joined at the tail with the skin on.
  2. Lay each fillet skin down and score it in 1 cm diamonds at 45 degrees, cutting through the flesh to the skin but never into it; salt, wine, and rest 10 minutes. 10 min
  3. Blot the fillets dry and rub starch down into every cut with your fingers, then shake them hard so the squares fall open and none stay glued shut.
  4. Hold the tail and lower a fillet into 180C (350F) oil so the squares spring open into spikes, let go and fry 4 minutes until rigid and pale gold, then lift and return it for 40 seconds at 200C (390F) until it rings when tapped. 4 min
  5. Boil the sugar, vinegar, tomato paste, soy and water, thicken with a starch slurry until it ribbons off the ladle and coats the back of it, stir in the peas, bamboo and pine nuts and pour it over at the table for the hiss.

From the pass

Cut to the skin and never through it. Skin contracts hard in the oil while the scored blocks do not, and that difference is what stands the squares up. Cut through and the fillet shreds as it fries; stop short of the skin and it comes out flat. Starch has to reach the bottom of every cut, or the wet faces weld back together and the fish fries as a plank.
