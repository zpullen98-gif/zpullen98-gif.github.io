Swedish · Bread

Knäckebröd

  • Intermediate
  • 35 min
  • $ roughly 1 of 4 for cost
  • Vegetarian
  • Vegan

earthy · charred Leads with an earthy, grounding base, char used as punctuation.

Ingredients

  • 300 g coarse wholemeal rye flour, plus more for rolling
  • 150 g strong white flour
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 9 g fine salt
  • 10 g caraway or fennel seed, lightly crushed
  • 300 ml water at 40C (104F)

Method

  1. Stir the yeast into the warm water, then work in both flours, the salt and the seed to a stiff, slightly tacky dough; cover and leave until visibly puffed.
  2. Divide into eight, and roll each piece on a rye dusted bench until you can see the grain of the wood through it, about 2 mm.
  3. Dock the whole surface hard with a fork or a knobbly pin, then cut a coin sized hole out of the centre of each round.
  4. Bake at 250C (480F) for 6 to 8 minutes, until the sheets are dry, blistered and freckled dark at the high points. 8 min
  5. Slide onto a rack at once and leave until stone cold; they snap rather than bend when they are done.

From the pass

Rye brings the water holding pentosans but not the gluten to trap gas, so this is not a raised bread; it is a sheet dried through. Roll to 2 mm or the middle stays leathery and goes limp inside a day. Dock it all over: undocked, steam pockets balloon the sheet into pillows that scorch on the domes and stay raw between them. Cool on a rack, never on the tray, or trapped steam softens the snap.
