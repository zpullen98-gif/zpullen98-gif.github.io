Emilia-Romagna · Dessert

Zuppa Inglese

  • Intermediate
  • 45 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$
  • Vegetarian

rich · bright · fiery Leads with richness that coats and comforts, acid holding everything up to the light, on a slow, honest burn.

Ingredients

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 125 g caster sugar
  • 40 g farina 00
  • 1 strip unwaxed lemon zest
  • 25 g unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 300 g savoiardi, or day-old pan di Spagna cut in fingers
  • 150 ml alchermes
  • 50 ml water

Equipment

Grater / zester

Allergen screen

Found: dairy · egg

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Infuse the milk with the lemon zest at 80C (176F) for 10 minutes, then lift the zest out. 10 min
  2. Whisk the yolks, sugar and flour to a pale ribbon, temper in the hot milk, and cook it back over moderate heat, stirring into the corners of the pan, until it thickens and then blips once at 85C (185F).
  3. Divide the hot cream, beat the cocoa into one half until it is glossy and free of lumps, and press cling film onto the surface of both so no skin forms.
  4. Cut the alchermes with the water, dip each savoiardo in and out in a single second, and layer the biscuits with alternating pale and chocolate cream in a glass dish, finishing with cream.
  5. Chill at least 6 hours, until a spoon lifts a clean edge and the biscuit is moist through with no dry white core. 360 min

From the pass

Two things break this. A flour-thickened crema that never reaches a blip is still full of live amylase from the yolks, which digests the starch overnight and leaves you soup in the morning, so take it to 85C (185F) and let it bubble once. The second is the dip: savoiardi drink alarmingly fast, and more than a second in the alchermes gives you paste that will not hold a layer.
