Texas Smoked Sausage

  • Advanced
  • 4 h
  • $$$ roughly 3 of 4 for cost

smoky · gently hot · aromatic Leads with wood-smoke running underneath, a warm current of chile, opening aromatic and staying there.

Ingredients

  • 1.2kg coarse-ground pork and beef (70/30)
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp coarse black pepper
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne
  • 3 g curing salt number 1 (6.25 percent sodium nitrite), 0.25 percent of the meat, weighed on a scale reading to 0.01 g
  • 120ml cold water
  • Hog casings, soaked
  • White bread, pickles, onion, and mustard to serve

Method

  1. Mix the coarse-ground meat with all the seasonings and the cold water until it just binds.
  2. Stuff into the soaked hog casings and twist into links; do not pack too tight.
  3. Rest in the fridge overnight to develop flavor and dry the casing.
  4. Smoke at 110C over post oak 3-4 hours until the internal temperature reaches 71C (160F). 240 min
  5. Serve Central Texas meat-market style: on butcher paper with white bread, pickles, onion, and mustard.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the techniques it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Low & slow smoking 12 recipes

  • The smoke coming off is thin and blue, not white and rolling. White smoke is unburnt fuel and it deposits soot and acrid phenols; a fire drawing enough air to burn its own smoke is what perfumes rather than tars.
  • The chamber held its temperature within a narrow band for the whole cook, adjusted with the air intake rather than by adding fuel in armfuls. A pit swinging fifty degrees cooks the outside twice and the middle once.
  • The stall was expected and waited out or wrapped through deliberately, not chased with heat. A brisket that sits at 70C (158F) for three hours is losing exactly as much water as it needs to; the cook who turns the fire up renders it dry.
  • Doneness was judged by feel and by probe, never by the clock: the probe slides in with no resistance anywhere, and the number that matters is the one where the collagen has gone, not a time per kilo.
  • It rested long and warm, an hour or more, wrapped and out of any draught. Cut straight off the pit and the juice runs onto the board instead of staying in the slice.

Where it goes wrong The fire was fed for temperature rather than for combustion, so it smouldered: white smoke, a bitter deposit on the surface, and a swing in the chamber every time more fuel went on. Then the stall was read as a stall in progress rather than evaporation doing its work, the heat went up, and the meat finished dry with a hard grey band under the bark.

The overnight rest: letting a dish marry 29 recipes

  • Cooled quickly before it went in (spread out, decanted, or stood in cold water) rather than carried to the fridge hot in the pot it was cooked in.
  • Protected from the rest of the fridge once cold: covered, or given space where nothing strong is sitting beside it. A rest this long is long enough to take on whatever it shares the shelf with, and some dishes want the open air.
  • Given the whole time. This is the one technique whose entire content is waiting, and cutting it short leaves the dish exactly as it was when it went in.
  • Brought back deliberately: reheated gently, or brought up to the temperature it is meant to be eaten at, rather than served straight out of the cold because it is late.
  • Tasted and corrected AFTER the rest. Seasoning and brightness both flatten in the cold, so the dish is finished at the end of the wait rather than at the start of it.

Where it goes wrong It went into the fridge hot and covered. It held its own heat for hours in the range where food spoils, and the lid sweated and dripped back into it all night: what comes out is duller and wetter than what went in, which is the exact opposite of the point.

From the pass

The German and Czech meat markets of Central Texas built the barbecue tradition, and sausage was their signature. Coarse grind and post oak are the Texas markers, and the overnight rest is what lets the casing dry so it snaps.
