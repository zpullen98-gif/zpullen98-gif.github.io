Appalachian Apple Stack Cake

  • Advanced
  • 4 h
  • $$ roughly 2 of 4 for cost
  • Vegetarian

rich · warm-spiced · aromatic · sweet-edged Leads with richness that coats and comforts, warm spice humming through the middle, trailing gentle sweetness.

Ingredients

  • Dried apples: 300g, simmered in water 45 min with ½ tsp cinnamon, cloves and allspice, then mashed to a thick, dark butter. Fresh apples are the wrong flavor; dried is the identity
  • Cake layers: 350g flour, 150g sugar, 120g butter, 120ml sorghum or molasses, 1 egg, 120ml buttermilk, 1 tsp soda, ginger
  • 5–7 THIN layers, baked in a skillet or cake pans: more crust than crumb, closer to soft gingerbread cookies
  • Patience: assembled, wrapped, and RESTED 2 days minimum
  • Only a dusting of powdered sugar on serving day, no icing; the apples are the frosting

Method

  1. Simmer and mash the dried apples with spices to a spreadable dark butter; cool.
  2. Make a stiff sorghum dough; divide in 6; pat each thin into a greased skillet and bake 180°C, 10–12 min: the layers come out firm and unpromising; trust the process. 12 min
  3. Stack: layer, thick apple spread, layer, until crowned; wrap the whole cake tight.
  4. Refrigerate 2–3 DAYS: the apple butter migrates and the firm layers soften into velvet. Dust and slice thin: it is richer than it admits.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the technique it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Mashing & puréeing 114 recipes

  • Smooth to the degree the dish asks for, judged on the tongue rather than by eye: no lumps, no fibres, no grit, unless texture is the point.
  • No gluey elasticity: a spoonful drops cleanly off the spoon rather than stretching in a rope behind it.
  • The consistency holds: it mounds on a spoon and stays there, neither slumping flat nor standing so stiff that a spoon has to cut it.
  • Nothing separates on the plate: no water or fat weeping out into a ring within a minute of serving.
  • The colour is even throughout, with no streaks of unincorporated fat, liquid, or seasoning at the edges of the bowl.

Where it goes wrong A starchy ingredient met a high-speed blade, so the cells burst and released their starch: what should be light comes out as an elastic glue that lifts off the spoon in one mass.

From the pass

The East Tennessee mountain wedding cake: neighbors each baked a layer and the bride's family provided the apples; the taller the stack, the more beloved the couple. Day one it is inedible discs and jam; day three it is one unified, spiced, apple-soaked marvel, which is either a baking lesson or a marriage metaphor, and in the mountains they will tell you it is both.
