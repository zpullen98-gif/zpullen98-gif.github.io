Eastern European · Soup

Barszcz

  • Intermediate
  • 1 h 30 m
  • Serves 4
  • $$$
  • Vegetarian

earthy · rich · herb-fresh · warm-spiced Leads with an earthy, grounding base, richness that coats and comforts, fading in warm spice.

Ingredients

  • 5 beets, 3 roasted or raw-grated
  • Good vegetable or beef stock, 1.5L
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, bay, allspice berries
  • 2 tbsp vinegar or beet sour (kwas), 1 tsp sugar
  • Sour cream, dill; uszka dumplings if it is Christmas

Equipment

Grater / zester

The skills inside

Straining & passing through a sieve

Allergen screen

Found: dairy

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Simmer beets and aromatics in stock 40 min. 40 min
  2. Strain for clear barszcz (Polish, ruby consommé) or blend for the thick road.
  3. Balance sharply: vinegar and sugar until it bites and hums.
  4. Serve with sour cream and dill, or crystal-clear with tiny mushroom uszka.

How it should come out

  • Deep luminous garnet; a spoonful held to the light reads red-purple, not brick, brown or dusty pink.
  • The strained version is genuinely clear: light passes through the bowl with no cloud or sediment; the blended version is uniformly smooth with no fibrous beet threads.
  • Nothing rides the surface and nothing strays into the bowl: no fat beads, no scum, no loose allspice berry or bay fragment; the only solids present are the ones put there deliberately.
  • Hot enough to steam but off the boil: a spoon of sour cream stirred through blends to an even pink without curdling into flecks or beading at the rim.
  • Sour first and hard enough to tighten the jaw, sugar arriving a beat behind to round it, salt last; if the first spoon reads sweet earth before it reads sour, it is not barszcz yet.

Where it goes wrong The vinegar went in early and the pot kept boiling, so the soup arrives a dull brownish brick with the sourness cooked flat, tasting merely of sweet earth.

From the pass

The soup must be SOUR: that is what barszcz means, and the old method built the sourness from fermented beet kvass before vinegar became the shortcut. Add the acid off the boil to keep the color garnet; long boiling turns beets the color of regret, because the pigment breaks down with heat and time. Clear barszcz with uszka dumplings is the Christmas Eve formal version; the everyday bowl takes cream and a boiled potato without apology.
