Fıstıklı Baklava

  • Advanced
  • 2 h
  • Serves 4
  • $$
  • Vegetarian

rich · nutty-toasted · bright · sweet-edged Leads with richness that coats and comforts, toasted, nutty warmth throughout, trailing gentle sweetness.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack filo (fresh if you can find it), ~30 sheets
  • 250g melted clarified butter
  • 300g Antep pistachios, finely ground (bright green)
  • Syrup: 400g sugar, 300ml water, squeeze of lemon: NO honey in Turkish baklava
  • Pinch of ground pistachio to crown

Equipment

Sheet pan

The skills inside

Handling phyllo

Allergen screen

Found: gluten · dairy · nuts

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Butter a tray; lay 12 buttered sheets, then half the nuts, 6 sheets, remaining nuts, 12 sheets.
  2. Cut all the way through into diamonds BEFORE baking.
  3. Bake 170°C, 45–50 min, until deep gold to the bottom. 50 min
  4. Boil syrup 10 min; pour COLD syrup over HOT baklava: listen to it crackle. Rest 4 h. 10 min

From the pass

Hot pastry, cold syrup (or the reverse) never both hot: that is the difference between crisp layers and a sweet sponge, and the crackle when the syrup pours is the sound of it going right. Gaziantep would also like a word about honey: no. Turkish baklava syrup is sugar and lemon, and the flavor is the Antep pistachio's, bright green and ground fine. Cut all the way through before baking; baked filo shatters under a knife. The four-hour rest lets the syrup finish its slow descent.
