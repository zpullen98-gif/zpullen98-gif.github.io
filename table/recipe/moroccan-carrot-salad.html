Moroccan Carrot Salad

  • Easy
  • 20 min
  • Serves 4
  • $
  • Vegetarian
  • Vegan

bright · herb-fresh · earthy · aromatic Leads with acid holding everything up to the light, fresh herbs arguing happily on top, opening aromatic and staying there.

Ingredients

  • 500g carrots, sliced coins, boiled just-tender
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp paprika, pinch cayenne
  • Lemon juice, olive oil
  • Cilantro, parsley

The skills inside

The overnight rest: letting a dish marry

Allergen screen

None found among the 14 screened.

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Boil carrots 6–8 min; drain hot. 8 min
  2. Toss immediately with garlic, spices, lemon, oil.
  3. Rest 30 min minimum; better after a night. 30 min
  4. Shower with herbs to serve, room temperature.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the technique it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

The overnight rest: letting a dish marry 27 recipes

  • Cooled quickly before it went in (spread out, decanted, or stood in cold water) rather than carried to the fridge hot in the pot it was cooked in.
  • Protected from the rest of the fridge once cold: covered, or given space where nothing strong is sitting beside it. A rest this long is long enough to take on whatever it shares the shelf with, and some dishes want the open air.
  • Given the whole time. This is the one technique whose entire content is waiting, and cutting it short leaves the dish exactly as it was when it went in.
  • Brought back deliberately: reheated gently, or brought up to the temperature it is meant to be eaten at, rather than served straight out of the cold because it is late.
  • Tasted and corrected AFTER the rest. Seasoning and brightness both flatten in the cold, so the dish is finished at the end of the wait rather than at the start of it.

Where it goes wrong It went into the fridge hot and covered. It held its own heat for hours in the range where food spoils, and the lid sweated and dripped back into it all night: what comes out is duller and wetter than what went in, which is the exact opposite of the point.

From the pass

Warm vegetables drink dressing: the same trick as the Niçoise potatoes, wearing a djellaba; toss the carrots with the cumin, lemon and oil straight from the colander and the seasoning penetrates instead of coating. The overnight rest is where good becomes serious. Cumin and carrot is one of the old reliable marriages of the spice road. Serve it room temperature among other little salads; cold from the fridge, half its perfume stays asleep.
