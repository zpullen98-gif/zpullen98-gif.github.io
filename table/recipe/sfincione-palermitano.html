Sicilian · Bread

Sfincione Palermitano

  • Intermediate
  • 45 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$

umami-deep · briny · rich Leads with savory depth stacked from the pantry’s bass notes, a briny, maritime salinity, on a rich, rounded finish.

Ingredients

  • 400g strong bread flour
  • 100g semola rimacinata di grano duro
  • 400ml water at 20C (68F)
  • 7g fresh yeast
  • 12g fine salt
  • 30ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 600g white onions, sliced thin
  • 200g peeled plum tomatoes, crushed
  • 8 salted anchovy fillets, rinsed
  • 150g primosale or young caciocavallo, diced
  • 60g coarse dry breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, for the topping
  • salt and black pepper

Equipment

Sheet pan

Allergen screen

Found: gluten · fish

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Mix the flours with 350ml of the water and rest 30 minutes until no dry flour remains, then work in the yeast, salt, oil and the last of the water until the dough is slack, sticky and peels off the bowl in sheets. 30 min
  2. Fold it in the bowl four times at 30 minute intervals, until it holds a low dome, then refrigerate overnight. 30 min
  3. Cook the onions in the topping oil over low heat for 40 minutes until jammy, pale gold and sweet with no bite left, melt in the anchovies, then add the tomato and reduce until the mix mounds on the spoon. 40 min
  4. Press the dough into an oiled 30x40cm tray and prove 2 hours at room temperature, until it reaches the corners and a poked dent fills back only halfway. 120 min
  5. Scatter the cheese, lay the onion on in spoonfuls without pressing, then the breadcrumbs and oregano, and bake on the lowest shelf at 220C (430F) for 25 minutes, until the base is crisp when lifted with a palette knife and the crumb topping is dark gold. 25 min

From the pass

Sfincione means sponge, and that crumb comes from 80 percent hydration plus a slow cold proof: the wetter the dough, the larger and thinner walled the gas cells. Dress it only after the pan proof, laying the topping on rather than pressing, or you crush the structure you waited overnight for. Breadcrumb goes over the onion last so it drinks the onion water and the top roasts instead of steaming.
