American · Bread

Skillet Cornbread

  • Easy
  • 35 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$
  • Vegetarian

rich Leads with richness that coats and comforts.

Ingredients

  • 240g cornmeal (+ 60g flour, or go 100% corn, Southern-style)
  • 1 tbsp baking powder, ½ tsp soda, 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs, 400ml buttermilk
  • 80g butter or bacon fat, melted in the skillet
  • Sugar: 0–2 tbsp, depending which side of the Mason-Dixon you cook for

Equipment

Skillet

Allergen screen

Found: gluten · dairy · egg

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Put the empty cast-iron skillet in a 220°C oven with the fat.
  2. Whisk wet into dry just barely.
  3. Pour batter into the screaming-hot fat: it must sizzle on contact.
  4. Bake 20–22 min; flip out crust-side up. 22 min

From the pass

The preheated skillet fries the bottom into a crust. Cornbread poured into a cold pan is just corn cake; the batter must sizzle on contact, which is why the empty cast iron heats in the oven with the fat already in it. Bacon fat over butter if the question ever arises. The sugar line is the true Mason-Dixon: Northern cornbread is sweet cake, Southern is savory bread, and the recipe's 0-2 tablespoon range is a diplomatic instrument. Flip it out crust-side up and serve it proud.
