North Carolina · Soup

Carolina Fish Stew

  • Intermediate
  • 75 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$

gently hot Leads with a warm current of chile.

Ingredients

  • 1kg firm white fish (rockfish or catfish), in large pieces
  • 150g streaky bacon, diced
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 800g potatoes, in thick slices
  • 400g crushed tomatoes
  • 1L water or fish stock
  • 4 eggs (optional, for poaching)
  • 2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt, black pepper, and hot sauce

The skills inside

Allergen screen

Found: egg · fish

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Render the bacon in a heavy pot; lift out the bits.
  2. Layer the onions and potatoes in the pot, add the tomatoes, water, and red pepper, and simmer 25 min until the potatoes soften. 25 min
  3. Lay the fish on top, cover, and poach gently 12 min without stirring; stirring breaks the fish. 12 min
  4. Crack the eggs onto the surface if using and poach 4 min in the steam. 4 min
  5. Season hard, scatter the bacon over, and ladle out carefully so the fish stays whole.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the techniques it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Rendering fat: and cooking in what runs out 53 recipes

  • It started cold or low and came up slowly: fat that met a hot pan seizes and scorches before the inside can melt, and there is no recovering it.
  • The fat in the pan is clear and clean-smelling, pale to golden, with no dark sediment on the floor and nothing acrid in the steam.
  • What is left behind is crisp and deep gold with no soft pale fat still clinging to it and no black burnt edges.
  • There is a real volume of it: enough to film the pan floor and be poured off and kept, not a dry smear that never ran.
  • Whatever cooks in it browns in it and takes its flavour, rather than sitting in a greasy pool that never gets hot enough to colour anything.

Where it goes wrong The heat was raised to hurry it along, so the outside seized and browned before the interior fat could melt; very little runs out, and the solids come out burnt on the outside and rubbery within.

The bare simmer: holding liquid below the boil 59 recipes

  • The surface is read rather than assumed: bubbles break slowly in one or two places, or the surface merely shivers, and it does not reach a rolling boil at any point.
  • It was held there: the pot was watched and the heat adjusted rather than set once and left, and there is no tide mark up the pot wall from a boil-over.
  • The liquid stayed as clear as it should be: no clouding from agitation, and no fat churned into an emulsion through it.
  • Whatever is in the liquid is still intact: nothing shredded, broken up, or worried apart by movement.
  • The level is roughly where the elapsed time should have left it; a large drop is the evidence that it ran hotter than this while nobody was looking.

Where it goes wrong It was brought to a boil and then left on the same heat, so it churned for the whole cook: the liquid clouded, the fat emulsified into it, and the solids broke apart into the broth.

From the pass

An Eastern Carolina river-camp dish, built in layers and never stirred once the fish goes in. The eggs poached on top are the old riverbank touch, and they are better than they sound.
