Benedictine Spread

  • Easy
  • 20 min
  • $$ roughly 2 of 4 for cost
  • Vegetarian

rich · bright Leads with richness that coats and comforts, acid holding everything up to the light.

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded, grated
  • 250g cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp grated onion
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and white pepper
  • A drop of green food coloring (traditional, optional)
  • White bread, crusts off

Equipment

Grater / zester

Method

  1. Grate the cucumber and onion, then squeeze both hard in a cloth to remove all their water: this is the crucial step.
  2. Beat the cream cheese smooth with the mayonnaise and lemon.
  3. Fold in the drained cucumber and onion.
  4. Season with salt and white pepper, and tint the palest green if you want the traditional look.
  5. Chill an hour. Spread on crustless white bread for tea sandwiches, or serve as a dip.

From the pass

Created by Louisville caterer Jennie Benedict around 1900. Wringing every drop of water from the cucumber is the difference between a spread and a puddle: a watery benedictine soaks the bread to mush within minutes.
