Raita

  • Easy
  • 10 min
  • Serves 4
  • $
  • Vegetarian

bright · earthy · gently hot · herb-fresh Leads with acid holding everything up to the light, an earthy, grounding base, lifted green at the very end.

Ingredients

  • 400g whole-milk yogurt, whisked
  • 1 cucumber, grated and squeezed
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin, ground
  • Mint or cilantro
  • Salt, pinch chili powder

Equipment

Grater / zester

The skills inside

Salting to draw the water out

Allergen screen

Found: dairy

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Whisk yogurt smooth with a splash of water.
  2. Squeeze the cucumber like you mean it.
  3. Fold everything together; dust with cumin and chili.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the technique it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Salting to draw the water out 23 recipes

  • Salted evenly over every surface rather than tipped on in one place and tossed once.
  • Laid out so the water has somewhere to go: a colander, a rack, or a sieve over a bowl, never a flat dish where it sits back in what it gave up.
  • Given real time, and there is visible liquid to show for it: a pool underneath, a drip, or a wrung handful.
  • That liquid is removed. It is squeezed, blotted or poured away, and the food is dry to the touch before it goes anywhere near heat.
  • The dish is seasoned knowing salt has already gone on, tasted before any more is added.

Where it goes wrong It went into the dish still wet, and was salted again out of habit. The water it was carrying dilutes and steams whatever it meets, so the result is watery and over-seasoned at once, and those are two faults that cannot be corrected in the same direction.

From the pass

Roast whole cumin in a dry pan, then grind: pre-ground is a photocopy of the smell; the toasting rebuilds the volatile oils that left the jar months ago. Squeeze the cucumber like you mean it, because its water is the difference between raita and yogurt soup by the time dinner is served. The dish's job at the table is thermal: yogurt's proteins bind capsaicin in a way water can't, which is why it sits beside every fiery plate on the subcontinent.
