Scandinavian · Starter

Toast Skagen

  • Easy
  • 20 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$$

rich · bright · herb-fresh Leads with richness that coats and comforts, acid holding everything up to the light, lifted green at the very end.

Ingredients

  • 300g cooked cold-water shrimp, peeled (the tiny sweet ones)
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise + 2 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 1 tbsp Dijon, plenty of chopped dill, lemon juice, white pepper
  • 4 slices white bread, crusts off, fried gold in butter
  • Vendace or lumpfish roe to crown; a lemon wedge and a glass of something cold

Allergen screen

Found: gluten · dairy · egg · fish · shellfish · mustard

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Drain the shrimp seriously: wet shrimp drown the dressing.
  2. Fold shrimp with mayo, crème fraîche, mustard, dill, lemon; season.
  3. Butter-fry the bread to an even gold; cool 1 min so it stays crisp. 1 min
  4. Pile the skagenröra generously; crown with a spoon of roe and a dill sprig.

From the pass

Invented by restaurateur Tore Wretman, who named it after a fishing port mid-regatta. The butter-fried bread is the difference between an elegant Swedish starter and shrimp salad on toast. Never skip the fry.
