Sugar on Snow

  • Easy
  • 20 min
  • Serves 4
  • $
  • Vegetarian
  • Vegan

sweet-edged Leads with a sweet edge rounding the corners.

Ingredients

  • 500ml pure maple syrup
  • A tray of clean packed snow or crushed ice
  • Sour pickles and plain doughnuts, to serve

Equipment

Thermometer · Sheet pan

The skills inside

Sugar stages & caramel

Allergen screen

None found among the 14 screened.

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Pack a tray with clean snow and set it in the freezer while you work.
  2. Boil the syrup in a deep pot, it foams high, until a candy thermometer reads 115C.
  3. Take the tray out and drizzle the hot syrup in ribbons across the snow.
  4. It sets in seconds into chewy taffy; lift it with a fork and twirl.
  5. Serve with a sour pickle and a plain doughnut on the side.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the technique it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Sugar stages & caramel 41 recipes

  • Nothing is stirred once it is boiling, and any crystals clinging to the side of the pan are brushed down rather than left to fall in.
  • Judged by temperature or by a tested stage rather than by time: consecutive stages are a few degrees apart and look identical in the pan.
  • Colour is read off the heat and in good light, remembering that sugar keeps darkening after the flame is out.
  • Stopped decisively at the point wanted: off the heat and into the next liquid or onto a cold surface, rather than left to coast in a hot pan.
  • The result is clear and smooth rather than grainy, cloudy, or seized into a solid mass.

Where it goes wrong It was stirred after coming to the boil, or a stray crystal from the side of the pan fell back in. One crystal seeds the whole pan and the syrup turns grainy within seconds, and once sugar has crystallised the only cure is more water and starting the whole thing again.

From the pass

The pickle is not a joke: the sourness cuts the sugar so you can eat more of it, which is the entire point of a sugaring party: sugar on snow, pickle, plain donut, repeat, in that order, until the season's syrup is properly honored. Boil the syrup to the thread stage and ribbon it over packed clean snow, where it sets into taffy you eat off a fork. Vermont has thrown these parties every March since the sugarhouses had neighbors, and the menu has not changed because it is already right.
