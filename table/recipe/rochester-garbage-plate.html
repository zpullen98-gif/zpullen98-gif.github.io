Rochester Garbage Plate

  • Intermediate
  • 75 min
  • Serves 4
  • $$

gently hot · earthy · rich · warm-spiced Leads with a warm current of chile, an earthy, grounding base, fading in warm spice.

Ingredients

  • 500g home fries, fried crisp
  • 500g macaroni salad
  • 4 cheeseburger patties or 4 red hots
  • For the hot sauce: 450g ground beef, 1 onion grated, 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp allspice, 1 tsp paprika, 250ml water
  • Yellow mustard, chopped onion, and white bread to serve

Equipment

Grater / zester

Allergen screen

Found: gluten · mustard

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Simmer the raw ground beef in the water with the onion and spices 45 min until dark and loose; never brown it first. 45 min
  2. Fry the home fries until crisp and make or buy a proper mayonnaise macaroni salad.
  3. Cover half a plate with home fries and the other half with macaroni salad.
  4. Lay the burgers or hot dogs across both.
  5. Ladle the meat sauce over everything, then mustard and raw onion. Two slices of white bread on the side, no argument.

From the pass

Nick Tahou Hots invented it and students perfected the timing, which is late; the Garbage Plate is Rochester's 2 a.m. institution, trademarked name and all, and every rival diner sells a 'trash plate' in tribute-by-legal-necessity. The architecture is fixed: home fries and macaroni salad as the foundation, meat on top, mustard and onions, and the meat hot sauce, a cumin-spiced fine-ground chili, over everything. Assembled hot, eaten immediately with a fork, mixing as you go. Regret is scheduled for morning.
