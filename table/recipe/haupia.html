Pacific & Hawaiian · Dessert

Haupia

  • Easy
  • 25 min
  • Serves 4
  • $
  • Vegetarian
  • Vegan

rich · fiery Leads with richness that coats and comforts, real fire that builds and stays.

Ingredients

  • 800ml coconut milk (two cans, full-fat: this dessert is coconut wearing a suit)
  • 80g sugar; pinch of salt
  • 60g cornstarch (the modern standard; pia, Polynesian arrowroot, is the ancestor)
  • 120ml water to slurry the starch
  • A square pan; cut into cubes when set, per luau law

Allergen screen

None found among the 14 screened.

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Warm coconut milk with sugar and salt.
  2. Whisk the cornstarch slurry in; cook over medium, whisking constantly, 5–8 min until it thickens to a heavy pudding that holds a trail. 8 min
  3. Pour into the pan; smooth; press film to the surface.
  4. Chill 3 h until firm; cut into neat squares. Serve cold, on ti leaf if you have it, or layer over chocolate in a pie shell for the famous chocolate haupia pie. 180 min

From the pass

Haupia sits at every luau in modest white squares and outlasts every fancier dessert on the table, firm enough to eat by hand, soft enough to vanish. The whisking window matters: undercooked slumps, overcooked bounces. And the chocolate haupia pie (a Ted's Bakery legend on Oahu's North Shore) is the officially sanctioned way to gild it.
