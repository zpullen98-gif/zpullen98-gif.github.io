Chicken & Sausage Jambalaya

  • Intermediate
  • 70 min
  • $ roughly 1 of 4 for cost

herb-fresh · aromatic Leads with fresh herbs arguing happily on top, aromatics blooming from the first bite.

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken thighs, 300g andouille
  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • The trinity + garlic
  • 800ml stock (Cajun) + optional 200g crushed tomato (Creole)
  • Cajun seasoning, bay, scallions

Method

  1. Brown sausage, then chicken, hard: the crust on the pot bottom is the flavor bank.
  2. Trinity in; scrape up every brown bit.
  3. Rice, stock, seasoning; boil, then lid on, lowest heat, 25 min. NO PEEKING. 25 min
  4. Rest 10, fold from the bottom, scallions over.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the technique it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Deglazing & pan sauces 49 recipes

  • The pan still holds its fond. Nothing has been rinsed, wiped, or soaked, and the browned layer is intact on the floor.
  • Excess fat was poured off first and the browned layer left behind; the fat is not the flavour and a sauce carrying all of it will not come together.
  • The liquid went into a still-hot pan and the floor came clean: scraped while it bubbles, the base goes from stuck brown to bare metal and the sauce darkens as it takes it up.
  • The fond was brown and not black. A burnt floor dissolves into bitterness that cannot be seasoned out, and is better washed away than deglazed.
  • Reduced first and seasoned afterwards, tasted at the consistency it will be served at rather than at the start.

Where it goes wrong The pan was left to stand and go cold before anybody came back to it. Cold fond will not dissolve, so the scraping lifts nothing, and the sauce arrives thin and pale with the flavour it was supposed to carry still welded to the metal.

From the pass

Brown equals Cajun, red equals Creole: both are correct and both camps will tell you the other is wrong; the tomato line runs straight through Louisiana and this recipe lets you pick a side. The crust the sausage and chicken leave on the pot bottom is the flavor bank, and the trinity's job is to rob it. Then the rice discipline: lid on, lowest heat, twenty-five minutes, no peeking: every lift of the lid bleeds the steam the rice is counting on. Fold from the bottom; the best grains live there.
