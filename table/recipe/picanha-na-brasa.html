Picanha na Brasa

  • Intermediate
  • 60 min
  • Serves 4
  • $

charred Leads with char used as punctuation.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole picanha (top sirloin cap / culotte), 1.2–1.5kg, fat cap fully intact: the cap IS the cut; a trimmed picanha is a demotion
  • Coarse rock salt (sal grosso), the churrasco seasoning in its entirety
  • Long skewers if going rodízio-style: cut with the grain into thick bands and fold into C-shapes, fat outward
  • Or the whole-roast road: fat cap scored in a diamond, seared cap-down first
  • Farofa, vinagrete (tomato-onion salsa), and grilled bread alongside

Equipment

Thermometer · Grill

The skills inside

Searing: the hard crustGrilling over live coalsRendering fat: and cooking in what runs outResting meat & slicing against the grain

Allergen screen

Found: gluten

Screened for: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, alcohol. Not screened: sulphites.

Method

  1. Salt generously just before the fire: rock salt crusts; excess brushes off after.
  2. Skewer road: fold the bands into Cs, fat on the outside curve; grill over medium-high coals, fat side catching first, turning until the crust is deep and the center rosy; SLICE the cooked outer layer off at the table and return the skewer to the fire, the rodízio rhythm.
  3. Whole road: sear cap-down until the fat renders and bronzes, 8–10 min; flip, move to indirect heat to 52–54°C internal; rest 10. 10 min
  4. Slice ACROSS the grain (the bands changed the grain direction: attend to it), each slice wearing its ribbon of golden fat. Vinagrete over; farofa for the juices.

How to tell it is going right

This dish has no standard of its own yet. It is judged on the techniques it exercises: checked at the pan, while there is still something to be done about it.

Resting meat & slicing against the grain 37 recipes

  • It came off the heat BEFORE the target temperature, measured with a probe rather than by time, because carryover keeps driving the inside up after it is out.
  • The probe went into the coldest likely part rather than the easiest one to reach: the reading that matters is the lowest one, not the first one.
  • Rested for a time matched to its size, somewhere warm, and not cut into partway through to see how it is doing.
  • The board is nearly dry when it is finally cut. A spreading pool means it was cut too early, and that liquid should have stayed inside.
  • The grain was found and the knife went ACROSS it, so the fibres are cut short, and the blade was sharp enough that the slices hold together rather than tearing.

Where it goes wrong It was cut straight off the heat, usually because everything else was ready. The muscle is still contracted and the juice still driven to the centre, so it runs out onto the board; the meat is drier than it ever needed to be, and the flavour that left it is sitting in a puddle under the knife.

Grilling over live coals 47 recipes

  • The fire was ready before the food was: coals evenly covered in grey ash with no flames licking through and no raw smoke coming off, and a hot zone and a cooler one both available.
  • The grate was hot and clean when the food landed, and the food released cleanly from it when the time came to turn rather than tearing away.
  • Marks are browned, not burnt: deep colour where the metal touched, with no carbon flaking off and no soot transferred onto the food.
  • The interior reaches its target with a thin gradient, not charred outside and cold within, and not driven through by a flare-up.
  • What comes off the fire smells of fire and food only: no raw wood smoke, no fuel, nothing acrid at the back of the nose.

Where it goes wrong It went on over live flames instead of settled coals, so the outside charred bitter in the flare-ups while the middle stayed cold, and everything on the plate tastes of soot.

From the pass

Brazil's crown cut, the sirloin cap that US butchers long buried inside 'top sirloin' until churrascarias taught everyone better. The fat cap bastes the meat from above as it renders, which is why removing it is treated as vandalism from Porto Alegre to São Paulo. The slice-and-return rodízio method means every serving is an edge piece; there is no polite way to say the middle-slice people were wrong, but they were.
